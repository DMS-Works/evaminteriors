Our Story

How It Began

For as long as I can remember, I have had an eye for detail, drawn to how a room can shift a person's experience from the moment you walk into it. I stumbled into interior design for the love of creating spaces and making them feel welcoming and warm. I have always been inspired by how layering different materials, shapes, colour and lighting can dramatically improve a space.

This passion for design has shaped every project I've worked on. Each space is an opportunity to incorporate elements of my own style into something uniquely tailored for each client.

Eva M Interiors

The Vision

At Eva M Interiors, we want our clients to feel the tangible impact a design gives, from transforming a bland space into something desirable where people genuinely enjoy being. We blend different materials and textures into our designs, understanding how people actually use and feel in a space. We tailor every design uniquely for our clients, while understanding their personal preferences. We complete each project to the highest standard, giving attention to every detail. From the first consultation to the completion of each project, we implement a clean, sleek finish while delivering exceptional quality and results.
