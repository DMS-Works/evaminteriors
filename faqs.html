Frequently asked questions

How much does a consultation cost, and what happens during it?

The consultation costs £75. It's a chance to discuss your project with a consultant, covering the type of project, pricing and budget, so we can understand exactly what you need.

Can I see examples of your previous work?

Yes, you'll find a small selection of completed projects in our portfolio here on the website. We're happy to share a wider range of our work with you after your consultation.

How long does a typical project take, from start to finish?

This depends largely on the size and scope of the project. Timelines vary from one project to the next, and we'll give you a clearer estimate during your consultation.

Do you visit my home, or can we work together virtually?

To get the best results, we prefer to visit your home in person. Virtual consultations are also available if that suits you better.

Do you source and buy furniture for me, or just recommend it?

Yes, we can do both. We can source and purchase furniture on your behalf, or simply recommend pieces for you to buy yourself. We'll discuss which option suits you during the consultation.

Will you work within my budget?

Yes, we always aim to work within your budget. If the scope of the project means the budget needs to increase, we'll explain why and talk it through with you first.

What's the difference between a mood board and full design execution?

A mood board gives you a sense of the ideas and direction for your project, while full design execution covers the styling and finishing touches that bring everything together.

I have a deadline in mind. Can you work to it?

Yes, we work hard to meet every deadline. If there are any delays, such as with furniture shipping, we'll let you know as soon as possible.

What areas do you cover?

We currently work across the UK, with in-person consultations available in London, Kent and Essex. Clients outside these areas can be supported virtually.

What happens if I'm not happy with the initial mood board direction?

You're welcome to share your thoughts and suggestions with your consultant. Every client receives up to two rounds of mood board revisions to make sure we get the direction right.
