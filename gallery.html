A room isn't created all at once. It's built through feeling, texture, contrast and detail.

Start with the feeling

Before choosing a single item, think about how you want the room to feel when you walk into it. Calm and restful? Warm and inviting? Bright and energising? This feeling becomes your guide for every decision that follows, from colours to furniture to lighting.

Build the foundation

This is where the big decisions happen: how the room is laid out, which large pieces of furniture you need, and what's underfoot, whether that's flooring, tiles or a rug. These choices are harder to change later, so it's worth getting them right first.

Layer colour & texture

Once the foundation is in place, bring in soft furnishings: cushions, throws, curtains and rugs. Mixing different materials, like linen, velvet and wool, adds warmth and makes a room feel finished rather than flat.

Add contrast & character

It's tempting to match everything perfectly, but a little contrast makes a room feel considered rather than showroom-flat. Try mixing light and dark tones, curved and straight shapes, or matte and shiny finishes.

Finish with the details

The final layer is what makes a space feel personal: lamps for softer lighting, a few books or objects that mean something to you, plants, and artwork. These small touches are what turn a house into your home.
